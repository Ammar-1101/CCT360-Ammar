A STORY IN MOTION

From Seed
to Forest

Every forest begins with something small.

01

It starts
with a seed.

Buried beneath the soil, a tiny seed waits. It looks insignificant, but everything the tree will become is already inside it.

02

Then,
it reaches.

With water, sunlight, and time, the first roots push downward while a small stem reaches toward the light.

03

Growth takes
time.

A tree does not become a forest overnight. Season after season, it grows stronger, taller, and wider.

04

One becomes
many.

Seeds fall. New trees emerge. What began as a single life becomes a connected ecosystem.

05

Everything
starts small.

A seed becomes a tree. A tree becomes a forest. And a small beginning can become something much larger.
